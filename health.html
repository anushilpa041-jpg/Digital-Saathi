Simple reminders and regular monitoring help seniors stay well and safe.

Daily Health Tips

  • Track blood pressure and sugar levels at the same time daily.
  • Take all medicines as prescribed and do not skip doses.
  • Watch for dizziness, fever, confusion, or unusual weakness.
  • Keep hydration and fiber in the diet.
