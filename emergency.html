Emergency Readiness

Preparedness can reduce panic and help seniors act quickly during sudden situations.

What to do in a crisis

  • Keep a charged mobile phone and emergency numbers visible.
  • If a fall occurs, do not try to stand immediately.
  • During heatwaves, stay hydrated and avoid direct sun.
  • In a power cut, keep flashlight, water, and a battery pack ready.

Emergency Kit

  • Flashlight and extra batteries
  • First-aid box
  • Glucose, prescribed medicines, and water
  • Emergency contact list and ID copy

Quick SOS

Press the emergency button to call 108 or keep a family contact ready.
