Daily Care Routine

A simple, steady routine helps seniors stay comfortable, active, and safe every day.

Morning Care

  • Wake up slowly and sit comfortably before standing.
  • Drink water and take medications on time.
  • Help with bathing, oral care, and getting dressed.
  • Open curtains and allow natural light for better energy.

Healthy Activity

  • Do light stretching or a short walk for 10–20 minutes.
  • Encourage balanced meals with fruits, vegetables, and protein.
  • Keep hydration in mind and avoid dehydration.
  • Take short rest breaks to prevent tiredness.

Afternoon & Evening

  • Take a rest after lunch and avoid overexertion.
  • Check feet, skin, and mobility for signs of discomfort.
  • Enjoy calm time, prayer, music, storytelling, or reading.
  • Prepare a comfortable bedtime routine with warm water and rest.

Care Checklist

  • Keep medicines in one labeled box.
  • Ensure walking paths are free from slippery surfaces.
  • Keep hearing aids, glasses, and cane within easy reach.
  • Have family check-in calls or daily monitoring.
